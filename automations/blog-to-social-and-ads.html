Blog to Social and Ads

Paste a blog link and tick a box. If it's worth posting, it comes back as LinkedIn, X and Facebook posts, each written to its platform's rules.

Read the runbook
01 · The order

A link, and a tick.

Paste a live blog URL into a row of the Notion database and tick Generate Posts. The database is checked every minute, and a row runs once: when its box is ticked and it hasn't been judged yet.

02 · The roll

The blog, read in full.

Jina Reader fetches the live page as plain text, and Claude reads the first 8,000 characters of it.

8,000 characters
03 · The test

Will it fly?

Before writing anything, Claude decides whether the blog is worth posting. It flies with a sharp insight, advice you can use today, a framework, a real story or a stat that challenges the usual thinking. Pure promotion, generic advice and lists of obvious tips stay on the ground.

04 · Three kites

One for each platform.

If it flies, Claude writes three posts at once. LinkedIn: a hook, 3 to 5 short paragraphs, a call to action and 3 hashtags, up to 1,200 characters. X: one sharp point and a hashtag, up to 230. Facebook: 2 or 3 warm sentences, no hashtags.

LinkedInXFacebook
05 · Up they go

Onto the blog's own page.

The three posts are added to the blog's Notion page under a Social Media Posts heading, LinkedIn first, then X, then Facebook, ready for someone to review.

06 · The flag

Marked post-worthy.

The row's Post Worthy field is set to yes. A blog that doesn't pass is marked no instead and gets no posts, so nobody has to wonder.

yesno
07 · The ad angles

Ads from the same blog.

Tick Create Ad Ideas as well, and the same blog is turned into ad ideas. Ad Idea Generated is ticked when they're done.

One blog in. Posts ready to go.

A blog link and a tick become LinkedIn, X and Facebook posts on the blog's own Notion page, or a clear no if it isn't worth posting.

01Problem

Every blog deserves a post. Not every blog deserves three.

Turning a blog into social posts means reading it again, deciding whether it has anything worth sharing, then writing it three times over, because LinkedIn, X and Facebook each want something different. It's quick to skip, and slow to do well.

02Running it

  1. 01

    Add the blog

    Paste the live blog URL into a new row of the Notion database. Check it opens in a browser first.

  2. 02

    Tick Generate Posts

    The database is checked every minute; the row is picked up on the next check.

  3. 03

    Wait a minute

    The blog is read, judged and, if it passes, written up three ways.

  4. 04

    Check Post Worthy

    Yes: open the row's page and review the three posts. No: the blog didn't pass the test, and nothing was written.

  5. 05

    Ad ideas, too

    Tick Create Ad Ideas on the same row for ad ideas from the blog. Ad Idea Generated is ticked when they're done.

04The posts

Every blog gets the same test, and every post the same rules for its platform, so the three always read like they belong where they're going.

05If something fails

  • Start hereThe workflow's Executions tab in n8n shows which step stopped and why.
  • Post Worthy stays emptyThe run stopped. Most often the URL isn't live or public: open it in a browser, fix it, then tick the box again.
  • It says noThe blog didn't pass the test. That's an answer, not an error: nothing was written.
  • Running a row againClear Post Worthy; the next change to the row runs it again. It adds a second set of posts under the first.
  • A long blog is only partly usedClaude reads the first 8,000 characters of the page's text.

06Outcome

~1 minuntil a ticked row is picked up
3posts for every blog that passes, one per platform
1clear yes or no for every blog

Every blog gets considered for social, the good ones come back ready to review, and the rest are marked so nobody spends time on them.

07Operating it

  • Once per blogA row with Post Worthy set is skipped, however often it's edited.
  • Live links onlyThe page is read from the web, so drafts and private links can't be used.
  • A review, not a postThe posts land on the Notion page. Nothing is published anywhere.
  • Two boxes, two jobsGenerate Posts makes the social posts; Create Ad Ideas makes the ad ideas.
