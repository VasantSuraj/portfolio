Blog Refresh Engine

Pick a webinar. Every live blog post is checked against what was said in it, and the ones worth refreshing come back ranked, each with a verdict and a brief.

Read the runbook
01 · Kept in step

Webinars and posts, synced every 6 hours.

The channel's webinars (livestreams of 33 minutes or more) and the blog's post list are copied into sheets. A post's page is fetched when it's new or has changed, and checked again every three days. A post that's gone is marked, never deleted.

02 · Cuttings first

Takeaways, with the words to prove them.

Pick a webinar (or a research report) in the app. The transcript is fetched, and Gemini pulls out 4 to 8 specific takeaways, each with a quote under 30 words and a timestamp. They're saved, so each webinar is only read once.

T1 … T6
03 · Down the rows

Every post ranked. 60, then 12.

A free keyword pass scores every live post against the takeaways, counting a match in the title three times and in a heading twice. The top 60 go to one quick Gemini call, which picks the 12 worth reading in full.

top 60the 12
04 · A close look

Scored 0 to 10, strictly.

Gemini reads each of the 12 in full. Most posts score under 6, and one that already covers the takeaway can't score above 5. For each takeaway it names the exact section it belongs in, what to add, and anything in the post it contradicts.

05 · Rank decides

Rewrite or optimise.

Ahrefs gives the post's best position for its keywords. Worse than #9, or ranking for nothing: rewrite, keeping the URL and rebuilding the body. Inside the top 9: optimise, adding to the sections that are already there.

rewriteoptimise
06 · The top six

Six posts, each with a reason.

The results are saved, and the app shows the six best fits with their scores, verdicts and why each was picked. A score of 8 or more is marked recommended.

07 · The care card

A brief a writer can follow.

For the post you pick, Gemini reads the top 10 search results, the post and the takeaways, and writes a brief section by section: keep, revise, replace or remove, with draft copy, new sections, SEO notes, FAQs and a checklist. It lands in a Google Doc shared with the editors.

One webinar in. Posts to refresh out.

Each webinar is matched against every live post. The ones worth refreshing come back ranked, with a verdict, a reason and a brief, and every two weeks their ranks are checked again.

01Problem

Every webinar says something new. The blog doesn't hear it.

A webinar is full of fresh points, numbers and quotes, and the blog has hundreds of older posts on the same subjects. Working out which posts a webinar should feed, where in each post the new point belongs, and whether a post needs a light touch or a rebuild means rereading the archive every time. So mostly it doesn't happen.

02Running it

  1. 01

    Let it sync

    Webinars and posts are copied into sheets every 6 hours. A sync can also be run from the app.

  2. 02

    Pick a webinar

    In the app, choose a webinar (or a research report) and run the comparison.

  3. 03

    Read the shortlist

    Six posts come back, each with a score, a verdict (rewrite or optimise) and the reason it was picked.

  4. 04

    Ask for a brief

    Pick one post. Its brief is written into a Google Doc, shared with the editors, and linked in the app.

  5. 05

    Refresh it

    A writer follows the brief. Every two weeks the ranks are checked again and the verdicts updated.

03Flow

  1. 01

    Keep the sheets current

    The channel's livestreams of 33 minutes or more go into a webinars sheet. The blog's post list goes into a posts sheet; a post's page is fetched when it's new, has changed or was last read over three days ago, up to 400 a run. Posts that disappear are marked removed.

    Schedule Trigger · HTTP Request · Google Sheets · Code
  2. 02

    Get the takeaways

    The app calls the workflow with a webinar. Its transcript is requested and checked every few seconds until it's ready. Gemini pulls 4 to 8 takeaways, each with a quote under 30 words and a timestamp, and they're saved for next time.

    Webhook · HTTP Request · Wait · If · Google Sheets
  3. 03

    Shortlist the posts

    Every live post is scored on keyword overlap with the takeaways: three points for the title, two for a heading, one for the rest. The top 60 go to one short Gemini call, which picks 12.

    Code · HTTP Request
  4. 04

    Read and score

    Gemini reads each of the 12 in full and scores the fit from 0 to 10, names the section each takeaway belongs in and what to add, and flags any contradiction.

    HTTP Request × 12 · Code
  5. 05

    Check the ranks

    Ahrefs returns each post's organic keywords (kept for 14 days). No keyword in the top 9 means rewrite; otherwise, optimise. A score of 8 or more is recommended.

    HTTP Request · Google Sheets
  6. 06

    Answer the app

    The results are saved, and the app gets the top six with their verdicts and reasons.

    Google Sheets · Respond to Webhook
  7. 07

    Write the brief

    For the chosen post: the top 10 search results for its keyword, the post's body and the takeaways go to Gemini. The brief is written into a new Google Doc, shared with the editors, and its link saved.

    Webhook · HTTP Request × 4 · Code · Google Sheets
  8. 08

    Recheck the ranks

    Every other Monday morning, each post with results gets fresh rankings and an updated verdict. Posts checked in the last 20 hours are skipped.

    Schedule Trigger · HTTP Request · Google Sheets

04The verdict

Two questions decide what happens to a post: how well the webinar fits it, and how well it already ranks. The score says whether it's worth touching; the rank says how hard.

05The brief

Every brief has the same parts, so a writer always knows where to look. It's written against the current top 10 results, not in a vacuum.

  • Section by sectionEach existing section marked keep, revise, replace, remove or merge, with draft copy where something changes.
  • New sectionsWhere the webinar adds something the post doesn't have.
  • Rewrite or optimiseA rewrite gets a full new outline. An optimise brief lists what not to touch.
  • ContradictionsAnything in the post the webinar now disagrees with.
  • SearchSEO notes from the top results, and an FAQ.
  • To finishRisks, a checklist and what success looks like.

06If something fails

  • Start hereThe Executions tab of the workflow in n8n shows which step stopped and why.
  • No takeaways come backThe transcript wasn't ready within the wait, about six minutes. Run the comparison again a little later.
  • A post never shows upIt's marked removed, or its page hasn't been fetched yet. Pages are fetched when new or changed, and reread every three days.
  • Every verdict says rewriteAhrefs returned no rankings. Check the Ahrefs connection, then run it again.
  • The editors can't open the briefThe Doc is shared with a fixed list of editors set in the workflow. Check that list.

07Outcome

08Operating it

  • Only live posts competeRemoved posts are marked, kept in the sheet and left out of every comparison.
  • Read onceA webinar's takeaways are saved; running it again reuses them.
  • Strict on purposeAn 8 means the webinar adds something real to that post.
  • A brief, not an editNothing on the blog is changed. A writer does the refresh.
