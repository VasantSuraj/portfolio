01Problem

A blog post was copied and pasted through too many places before it reached Webflow.

Writers moved each draft through several tools to edit out the AI feel, then rebuilt it in the Webflow CMS by hand: pasting the body, fixing the formatting, and re-entering the slug, meta title, meta description, thumbnail, author and category. That took the better part of an hour per post, fields were often missed, and every writer needed Webflow access and had to learn how its CMS works.

The brief: writers paste the post once, into a Notion board, and flip its status. The machine builds the Webflow draft. Writers never need Webflow; reviewers only review.

    Webflow · Code
  4. 04

    A blog post is created in the Webflow CMS as a draft with name, slug, meta title, meta description, thumbnail, author and category. The SEO values are fields in Notion, carried across as written.

04Guardrails

  • Two-flag triggerA run needs Approved to upload and Drafted to Webflow = No. The second flag flips to Yes after the push, so a drafted post can never be pushed again by accident.
  • Draft, never liveEvery post lands as a draft. A person reviews it from the Slack link and decides when it goes out.
  • Missing thumbnailOne of the common failures. Add the image in Notion and approve the post again.
  • Unmapped author or categoryA select with no matching Webflow reference item stops the run. Create the reference in Webflow first.
  • Slug already usedWebflow will not take a slug that exists. Change it in Notion and approve again.
  • Very long postsPosts of roughly 20 pages or more can hang the push, so they go to manual upload.

07Operating it

  • Push a postPaste the post into the Notion board and set Status to Approved to upload. The draft and the Slack link arrive a few minutes later.
  • Add an author or categoryCreate the matching reference item in Webflow, named exactly like the Notion select.
  • Fix a failed runCorrect the thumbnail, reference or slug in Notion, then approve the post again.
  • Very long postsAnything around 20 pages or more is uploaded by hand.
