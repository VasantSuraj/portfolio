Organic Social Engine

Post ideas come in from Reddit, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, the blog, sales calls and webinars. Each is written in the right voice, edited, reviewed and turned into a carousel or an image, all from one Notion board.

01 · The weekly freight

Six lines, sorted once a week.

Once a week, scrapers bring in the week's posts from Reddit, LinkedIn searches, a list of thought leaders, X and YouTube. A post goes on only with more than 30 likes, and only if Gemini judges it on topic. The blog's posts from the last 7 days come in too, each already drafted.

30+ likes, on topicthe rest
02 · The specials

Calls and webinars, any time.

Send a call transcript and Claude pulls out 3 to 5 post ideas, each with the voice it suits. Send a webinar and Claude writes three captions to promote it: a hook, three takeaways, a call to action and a question to end on. Both land on the board.

03 · The board

One row for each idea.

Every idea is a row on one Notion board, with where it came from, a summary and the voice it's assigned to. Tick Generate Content on a row: within a minute it's picked up, the box is cleared and the status becomes Generating.

New IdeaGenerating
04 · The sheds

Written in its own voice.

There's a writer for each of four voices, three people and the company account, each briefed on how that voice writes. Then the same voice's editor sharpens the hook and cuts what doesn't earn its place. The draft goes back on the row as In Review.

In Review
05 · The repair shop

Edits, from a note.

Not right yet? Write what to change in Edit Instructions and set the status to Needs Edits. Within a minute Claude revises the draft to the note, keeps the voice, clears the note and sets After Edit Instructions.

Needs EditsAfter Edit Instructions
06 · The paint shop

A carousel, or an image.

Pick a Content Type and set Generate Creative. A carousel comes back as five slides, a cover, three points and a call to action, each with its word limits, ready to design. An image post gets an image made for it, saved to Google Drive.

CarouselImage Post
07 · The starter

Creative ready.

Once the carousel's slides are on the row, the status becomes Creative Ready and the post is ready for someone to design and publish. Nothing is posted on its own.

Creative Ready

01Problem

A good post needs something worth saying, and a voice that sounds like its author.

Finding the first means reading Reddit, LinkedIn, X and YouTube every week and remembering what came up on calls and webinars. The second means writing each idea up in the right voice, three people's and the company's, then editing it, and then briefing the carousel. Each step is easy to skip.

02Running it

  1. 01

    Let the ideas come in

    The scrape runs once a week on its own. Send a call transcript or a webinar to its webhook whenever there's one.

  2. 02

    Pick an idea

    On the board, read the summary and check Assigned Profile: the voice it will be written in.

  3. 03

    Tick Generate Content

    Within a minute the row says Generating; soon after, In Review, with the post in Generated Draft.

  4. 04

    Review it

    Not right yet? Write Edit Instructions and set Needs Edits. It comes back as After Edit Instructions.

  5. 05

    Ask for the creative

    Set Content Type to Carousel or Image Post and the status to Generate Creative. A carousel's slides land in Creative Brief, marked Creative Ready.

03Flow

  1. 01

    The weekly scrape

    Apify scrapers fetch the week's top Reddit threads, LinkedIn search results, a list of thought leaders' posts and X posts; a YouTube search adds each video's views and likes. Posts with more than 30 likes go on, unless they're from a blocked handle.

    Schedule · Apify · YouTube · Filter
  2. 02

    Summarise and judge

    Gemini summarises each post, suggests a voice and says whether it's on topic. Off-topic posts are dropped.

    Gemini · Filter
  3. 03

    The week's blogs

    Blog posts from the last 7 days are read from a sheet, and Gemini drafts a post from each in the company's voice.

    Google Sheets · Gemini
  4. 04

    Onto the board

    Each idea becomes a row marked New Idea, with its source, link, summary and voice. A digest of the week's haul goes to Slack.

    Notion · Slack
  5. 05

    Calls and webinars

    One webhook takes a call transcript: Claude finds 3 to 5 ideas, each with a title, the insight and a voice. Another takes a webinar's title, summary and transcript: Claude writes three captions, saved on a row as a ready draft.

    Webhook · Claude · Notion
  6. 06

    Write, then edit

    Every minute, rows with Generate Content ticked are set to Generating and unticked. The row's voice picks its writer and its editor; the draft is saved in pieces under Notion's length limit and the row goes to In Review.

    Schedule · Switch · Claude · Notion
  7. 07

    Edits

    Every minute, rows at Needs Edits are revised to their Edit Instructions, which are then cleared, and set to After Edit Instructions.

    Schedule · Claude · Notion
  8. 08

    Creative

    Every minute, rows at Generate Creative are read by Content Type. A carousel: Claude breaks the draft into five slides, saves them to Creative Brief and marks the row Creative Ready. An image post: an image is generated and saved to Google Drive.

    Schedule · Switch · Claude · OpenAI · Google Drive

04The rules

Every step has the same brief every time, so what lands on the board is always in the same shape.

#PartThe ruleIn the yard
1The gateMore than 30 likes, judged on topic, not from a blocked handlethe hump and the spur
2Calls3 to 5 ideas, each a short title, a two-sentence insight and a voicethe call special
3WebinarsThree captions of 150 to 200 words: a hook, three takeaways, a call to action, a questionthe webinar special
4WritersOne for each voice, briefed on its topics, its tone and how long it runsthe four sheds
5EditorsThe same voice: a sharper first line, nothing generic, no commentary around the postthe second board in each shed
6CarouselA cover (8-word headline, 20-word subtext), three slides (8 and 25 words) and a call to actionthe five painted cars

05If something fails

  • Start hereEach workflow's Executions tab in n8n shows which step stopped and why.
  • A row stays at GeneratingIts Assigned Profile has to be one of the four voices; anything else matches no writer. Fix it, set the status back and tick the box again.
  • The week brought few ideasThe 30-like gate and the topic check may have kept most of them out. The Slack digest shows what came in.
  • Edits on a very long draftThe revised draft is saved in one piece, up to 2,000 characters.

06Outcome

A week of ideas arrives without anyone searching for them, and every one of them can be a reviewed draft in the right voice, with its carousel briefed, in a few minutes.

07Operating it

  • A review, not a postDrafts, briefs and images stay on the board and in Drive. Nothing is published anywhere.
  • The voice is set per rowChange Assigned Profile before ticking Generate Content.
  • One switch per stepGenerate Content for the draft, Needs Edits for changes, Generate Creative for the carousel or image.
  • Calls and webinars on demandThey come in when they're sent, not with the weekly scrape.
