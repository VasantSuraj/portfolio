01 · The brief

Just talk.

Open the recorder in its own browser tab, tap the mic and talk through the topic for two to five minutes: the argument, the problem, the takeaway, any numbers. Messy and conversational is fine.

02 · The hints

Three boxes, all optional.

Stop, and the recording plays back for a check. Add a title hint and a primary and secondary keyword if you like. Anything left blank is worked out from the recording.

blank means worked out
03 · Send

One button sends it all.

Send posts the audio and the hints to the workflow, with the time it was recorded. The station clock starts here.

04 · The words

Voice to text in seconds.

Groq transcribes the whole recording. The talk comes back as plain text, every word of it.

transcript
05 · The voice

Rewritten in the house voice.

Claude turns the transcript into a full, publish-ready post of 5,000 to 7,000 words: direct, data-led, written like an operator. When the hints were left blank, it writes the title and keywords too.

06 · Into Notion

Filed as a draft.

A Notion page is created with the title, the primary and secondary keyword, a 160-character meta description, the word count, the time it was recorded, and its status set to Draft.

07 · The ping

A direct message with the link.

About 60 to 90 seconds after Send, a Slack DM arrives with the title, keyword, word count and the Notion link. Read it, then move it from Draft to Review when it's ready for an editor.

01Problem

Most ideas for a post start out loud.

They come up in a call, on a walk or between meetings. Turning that into a written draft meant sitting down and typing it all up first, so a lot of good ideas never got written.

02Running it

  1. 01

    Open the recorder

    Open it in its own browser tab and allow microphone access the first time. Inside a Notion embed the microphone is blocked.

  2. 02

    Talk

    Tap the mic and talk through the topic for two to five minutes: the argument, the problem, the takeaway, any data or examples you want in it. Conversational is fine.

  3. 03

    Check it

    Tap the mic again to stop. The recording plays back so you can listen before sending.

  4. 04

    Add hints, or don't

    A title hint and a primary and secondary keyword are optional. Leave them blank and they're worked out from the recording.

  5. 05

    Send

    The audio, the hints and the time it was recorded go to the workflow.

  6. 06

    Wait for the DM

    Within 60 to 90 seconds a Slack direct message arrives with the Notion link.

  7. 07

    Review it

    Read the page in Notion and change its status from Draft to Review when it's ready for an editor.

03Flow

  1. 01

    The recording arrives

    The recorder posts the audio with the optional title hint and keywords and the time it was recorded. The webhook only accepts calls from the recorder's own page.

    Webhook
  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Groq runs Whisper (large-v3 Turbo) on the audio, in English, and returns the whole talk as text.

    HTTP Request · Groq
  3. 03

    Gather

    The transcript and the three hints are set side by side, ready for the writer.

    Set
  4. 04

    Write the post

    Claude rewrites the transcript as a complete post in the house voice and fills anything left blank. It returns the title, both keywords, a 160-character meta description, the word count and the post itself.

    Claude
  5. 05

    Take it apart

    The reply is read, and the post's markdown becomes Notion blocks: headings, bullets, numbered points and paragraphs, in batches of 99.

    Code × 2
  6. 06

    File it in Notion

    The first batch creates the page with its seven properties and the status at Draft. Any further batches are added to the same page, so a long post arrives whole.

    If · HTTP Request × 2
  7. 07

    Tell the person who recorded it

    A Slack direct message with the title, keyword, word count and the link to the Notion page.

    Merge · Slack

04The draft

The post always comes in the same order: a TL;DR at the top, a hook, question-led sections, "In a nutshell", then the FAQs. Every recording lands as one row in the drafts database, with these properties filled in.

05If something fails

  • Start hereThe Executions tab in n8n shows which step stopped and why.
  • The mic is blockedThe recorder was opened inside a Notion embed, whose sandbox blocks the microphone. Open it in its own browser tab.
  • Transcription returns 401The Groq key has been replaced. Create a new key and update the credential on the transcription step.
  • Notion returns 401The Notion token is wrong, or the integration has lost access to the database. Update the credential on both Notion steps and check the database's connections.

06Outcome

2–5 minof talking is all the input it needs
5–7kwords the draft is written to
60–90 sfrom Send to the link in Slack

A spoken brief becomes a full, reviewable draft in Notion without anyone typing it up. The writing time goes into editing, not into the first pass.

07Operating it

  • Knowing it's doneThe Slack DM with the Notion link arrives. Until then, the run is still going.
  • Set up onceThe recorder keeps the workflow's address in its settings panel, saved in your browser, so there's nothing to enter each time.
  • Only the recorderThe webhook accepts calls from the recorder's own page and nowhere else.
  • Always a draftPages land with their status at Draft. Moving them to Review and Published is done by hand.
